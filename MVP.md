# MVP

## Product promise

See how many jobs, how much revenue, or how many billable hours a small service business needs to cover costs—and what it takes to hit a monthly profit goal.

## Included

- Per-job and billable-hours modes
- Live contribution margin, break-even, and target-profit results
- Whole-job operational rounding with raw mathematical values
- Safe zero and negative contribution-margin states
- Integer-cent currency parsing and deterministic calculations
- Accessible, responsive, mobile-first controls
- Lightweight break-even progress explanation
- Versioned, debounced, browser-local draft recovery
- Privacy, Terms, Support, metadata, favicon, and JakeGenerates shell

## Completion checks

Lint, automated tests, production build, desktop browser review, and 390px mobile review must pass before release.
