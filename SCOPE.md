# Scope

## In scope

- Monthly fixed overhead and optional target profit
- Average selling price and variable cost per job
- Billable rate and variable labor cost per billable hour
- Contribution margin, break-even volume/revenue, and target-profit volume/revenue
- Local draft persistence and clear/reset control

## Out of scope

This is not accounting, bookkeeping, payroll, forecasting, quoting, invoicing, CRM, business-history, or customer-management software. It has no AI, API, authentication, database, cloud sync, integrations, PDF export, email, or payments.

## Calculation policy

Currency inputs are nonnegative, accept up to two decimal places, and become integer cents. Jobs round up for the primary operational requirement; hours remain decimal. Revenue is calculated from the raw required volume and rounded to cents. A zero or negative contribution margin has no reachable break-even result.
